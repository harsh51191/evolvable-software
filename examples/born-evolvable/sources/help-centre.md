# Help centre (source documents for the drafter)

These sections are the local source the AI drafter stand-in reads. It drafts answers from them by rule; it never invents text.

## Resetting your password
Choose 'Forgot password' on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you. The link works for one hour.

## Exporting invoices
Go to Billing, then Invoices, and choose Export. You can export every invoice for a date range as a CSV or PDF file.

## Password rules
A password must be at least 12 characters long. Administrators can change the password rules for the whole organisation under Security settings.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of approval.

## Opening hours
The support team is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UK time.
